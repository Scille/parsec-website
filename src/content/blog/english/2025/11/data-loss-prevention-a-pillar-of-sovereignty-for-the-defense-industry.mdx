### Introduction

In the defense industry, protecting information isn't simply a cybersecurity issue — it's a matter of national sovereignty. Companies in this sector handle highly sensitive data — weapons plans, R&D innovations, critical infrastructure, operational strategy — that make prime targets for economic or military espionage.
Preserving these strategic assets requires a sovereign, comprehensive, and proactive Data Loss Prevention (DLP) approach. It must simultaneously cover **securing internal exchanges**, **document traceability**, and **leak detection on clandestine networks**.

## 1. Securing sensitive exchanges with Zero Trust

R&D teams, engineers and suppliers work with extremely sensitive files.
Zero Trust security ensures that only authenticated, authorized people can access documents. Thanks to end-to-end encryption, even if a workstation or server is compromised, the data remains unreadable to any unauthorized party.

**Benefits:**

- Reduced risk of internal leaks
- Protection against attacks targeting supply chains
- Protection of intellectual property

## 2. Document traceability to guarantee the integrity of critical files

Even a minor alteration to a plan or technical drawing can have major consequences.
DLP seals a unique identifier, an integrity fingerprint, and proof of origin into every file. This "internal signature" makes it possible to instantly verify whether a document has been modified, and to distinguish the authentic version from any fraudulent copies.

**Benefits:**

- Immediate detection of unauthorized modifications
- Solid digital evidence in the event of an audit
- Protection against forgery and document-based deepfakes

## 3. Proactive leak detection on the Deep & Dark Web

Even the best-protected systems can be targeted. That's why DLP includes continuous monitoring of clandestine spaces.
If a strategic document, an identifier, a confidential drawing, or any sensitive information appears on these networks, an alert is sent immediately.

**Benefits:**

- Early identification of a leak or compromise
- Limiting the impact of a confirmed leak
- Stronger protection of national interests

### Conclusion

In a sector where every piece of information can carry geopolitical implications, sovereign DLP is a genuine safeguard. By combining Zero Trust security, document traceability, and advanced Deep & Dark Web monitoring, it offers complete risk coverage.
For the defense industry, it isn't just a technology solution — it's an instrument of national sovereignty and resilience.
