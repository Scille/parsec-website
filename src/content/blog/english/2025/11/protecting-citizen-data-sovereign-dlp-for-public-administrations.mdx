### Introduction

Public administrations and local authorities hold and produce an enormous amount of sensitive data: personal information, official decisions, internal reports, HR files, strategic land-use planning documents.
As cyberattacks against the public sector surge, and as NIS2 obligations take hold, guaranteeing the confidentiality, integrity, and monitoring of this data becomes an absolute priority. Sovereign Data Loss Prevention offers a robust response, tailored to institutional needs.

## 1. Zero Trust security to protect administrative data

The sensitivity of the data handled by public servants requires rigorous access control.
With a Zero Trust approach, only authorized users can view or handle documents. Files remain permanently encrypted, preventing access by any third party — even technical administrators.

**Benefits:**

- Drastically reduced risk of internal leaks
- Protection of personal data and confidential files
- Increased resilience against cyberattacks

## 2. Integrity and authenticity of official documents

The public sector must guarantee that published or transmitted documents are reliable and authentic.
Sealing a unique identifier into every file proves that a document hasn't been altered since it was issued.

This is particularly useful for:

- municipal orders,
- administrative decisions,
- meeting minutes,
- financial and budgetary documents.

**Benefits:**

- Fighting disinformation
- Greater transparency for citizens
- Increased trust in official communications

## 3. Deep & Dark Web monitoring to prevent compromise

Local authorities are regularly targeted by cyberattacks aiming to exploit their data.
DLP continuously monitors clandestine spaces and raises an alert whenever it detects the presence of:

- citizen data,
- staff credentials,
- stolen internal documents,
- databases exposed for resale.

**Benefits:**

- Rapid response in the event of a leak
- Limiting the impact on citizens
- Compliance with NIS2 requirements

### Conclusion

For the public sector, sovereign DLP is a lever for trust and continuity of service. It guarantees the integrity of official documents, secures citizen data, and strengthens resilience against digital threats.
By adopting a proactive, sovereign approach, administrations protect not only their systems, but also the trust of the citizens they serve.
