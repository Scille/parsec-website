System integrators play a key role in the digital transformation of companies. Their mission is to deploy robust, reliable and secure IT infrastructures, while keeping installation and hosting costs under control. However, in an increasingly competitive economic environment, it's becoming essential to offer solutions that combine performance, security and flexibility, without driving up the bill for either the integrator or the end customer.

By integrating PARSEC, you can control the costs associated with using the cloud in several ways:

1. **Reduce data transfer costs**

By encrypting data prior to transfer, you minimize the security risks associated with data exchange between employees and across multiple sites. Most of the time, these exchanges take place either via insecure channels, or via the installation of specific channels or tools, which can nevertheless generate significant costs.

With PARSEC, you no longer need to use more expensive transfer channels to guarantee confidentiality. A single application is all you need to both secure and share your sensitive documents, while relying on a less expensive cloud infrastructure. Indeed, thanks to data encryption, the physical security requirements of the cloud infrastructure are reduced. This means you can opt for less expensive cloud hosting services without compromising on security.

2. **Lower compliance costs**

By guaranteeing data protection at the terminal, PARSEC helps meet regulatory requirements. This avoids fines for non-compliance and reduces the cost of implementing additional security solutions.

3. Flexibility**and adaptability**

PARSEC's flexible architecture adapts to different cloud architectures (public, private, hybrid), enabling integrators in particular to choose the most economical hosting options without sacrificing security.

Also, by centralizing data security management in PARSEC, the costs associated with managing multiple security tools and protocols will be much lower, and data processing will be smoother, faster and more ergonomic for your employees.

**Parsec is a strategic asset for integrators**

By adopting Parsec, integrators can not only optimize installation and hosting costs, but also offer their customers a secure, scalable solution. Parsec guarantees data protection while offering the flexibility needed to adapt to the most complex projects.

Our solution helps you control costs while guaranteeing optimum security, a major asset for the success of your integration projects.
