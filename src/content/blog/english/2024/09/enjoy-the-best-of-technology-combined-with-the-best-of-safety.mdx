In the industrial sector, technological performance must go hand in hand with data protection. Production, patent, process and supply chain information are valuable assets that need to be protected against cyber threats. Industrial companies are prime targets for cyber attacks, as a data breach can lead to massive financial consequences, production stoppages or even theft of intellectual property.

**Securing industry-critical data**

Modern, increasingly connected industries face a major challenge: how can they take advantage of digital transformation without compromising the security of critical information? Industrial control systems,**Industry 4.0** technologies and the integration of new technologies into production processes increase the number of entry points for cyber attacks. A compromise of industrial systems can lead to costly production interruptions, or even threats to employee safety.

**PARSEC's response to industry challenges**

Parsec is the perfect answer to these needs, offering an advanced security solution to protect sensitive industrial information. Thanks to the innovative combination of ZeroKnowledge and ZeroTrust, PARSEC guarantees that data relating to production, technical plans or innovations can only be consulted by authorized persons.

This also means that even the cloud hosting provider, if storage is on servers external to the company, never has access to encryption keys or plaintext data. This ensures that stored information remains totally inaccessible to third parties, including the service operator itself.

This approach places data confidentiality at the heart of the solution, offering optimum protection and enhanced compliance with regulations such as the RGPD.

In addition, collaboration with external, and therefore potentially less secure, departments is almost inevitable in this sector. This collaboration, while necessary for development and production, must be carried out securely. PARSEC makes it possible to share critical information with partners, while guaranteeing that this data remains protected even outside the company walls, as all files saved in PARSEC are encrypted end-to-end before being exported from the user terminal to the storage cloud.

Thanks to this rigorous access management and technological innovation, PARSEC enables industries to collaborate with confidence, while ensuring the protection of their most valuable assets.

By adopting PARSEC, you can boost your company's competitiveness while guaranteeing the security of your processes.
