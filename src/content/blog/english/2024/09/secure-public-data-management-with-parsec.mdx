Public administrations manage a massive volume of sensitive data, from tax information to medical records and administrative data relating to citizens. The increasing digitization of public services calls for secure solutions to protect this information against cyber attacks. Administrations are subject to strict data protection requirements, and a breach could not only damage citizens' trust, but also jeopardize the smooth running of essential public services.

**Stringent safety requirements for public authorities**

Administrations must ensure the protection of the data of millions of citizens while complying with strict standards such as the **RGPD** and the **NIS 2** directive. The management of sensitive information must not only guarantee data confidentiality, but also enable complete traceability of interactions to ensure total transparency vis-à-vis the public.

Any compromise of public systems can have major repercussions, affecting citizens' trust in institutions and jeopardizing essential services such as social security, taxes or health services.

**Parsec: a tailor-made solution for public authorities**

With PARSEC, public administrations have a complete solution for securely managing citizen data. Parsec offers **end-to-end encryption**, ensuring that only authorized persons can access information. Every document is protected from creation to archiving, and any attempt at unauthorized access is immediately blocked.

What's more, Parsec lets you precisely manage **access rights** according to the roles of administrative agents. You can define who has access to data, what actions can be carried out (consultation, modification, sharing) and track every interaction thanks to complete traceability.

**Flexible, secure access management**

Public administrations are often organized into several distinct departments, and each department may need access to specific information. Parsec makes it possible to manage these different levels of access according to the needs of each department, ensuring that only authorized people have access to the relevant information.

What's more, PARSEC incorporates a **Zero Trust** security model, ensuring that every user is authenticated and verified. This guarantees enhanced protection, even for internal users.

**Regulatory compliance and strengthening public confidence**

By adopting PARSEC, administrations can demonstrate their commitment to protecting citizens' data. By ensuring compliance with regulations such as the **RGPD** and the **NIS 2** directive, PARSEC helps strengthen citizens' trust in public institutions. Every action is recorded and traceable, offering total transparency over the management of public information.

**Conclusion: PARSEC, a secure solution for public services**

For public administrations, PARSEC represents an essential solution for securing the management of sensitive data. By offering enhanced protection through end-to-end encryption and advanced management of access rights, PARSEC guarantees information security while ensuring full compliance with current regulations. With PARSEC, you can protect citizens' data while ensuring greater transparency and efficiency in the management of public services.
