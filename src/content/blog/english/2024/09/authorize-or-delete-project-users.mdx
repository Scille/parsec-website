In the banking and insurance sectors, managing access to sensitive data is a major concern. Whether it's customer financial information, insurance contracts or risk management documents, companies need to ensure that only authorized people can access data, and that access is rigorously controlled.

**The challenges of access management in the banking and insurance sectors**

in these sectors, legislation imposes strict data protection standards. The **RGPD**, but also regulations specific to the financial sector (such as the European **PSD2** directive for payment services), impose strict management of access to sensitive information. Companies must be able to demonstrate that they control precisely who can access data, and that they can revoke such access at any time, depending on project needs.

**Parsec: secure, flexible access management**

PARSEC offers a simple, secure access management solution to meet these challenges. With PARSEC , you can easily authorize or revoke access to data as projects evolve. Whether you want to add a new user to a workspace, or withdraw access from a former employee, you can manage these rights in just a few clicks, without compromising information security.

Each user has specific authorizations, defined according to his or her role and responsibilities. For example, a bank advisor may have access to customer data, but not to the information of other advisors. Similarly, a risk manager in an insurance company may consult certain documents, but not be able to modify or share them.

**Enhanced security with end-to-end encryption**

In addition to access management, Parsec provides **end-to-end** data **encryption**, ensuring that only authorized persons can view or modify information. This means that even if data is intercepted during transfer to the storage cloud, it remains unreadable to unauthorized third parties.

This level of protection is particularly important in the banking and insurance sectors, where customers' financial and personal data must be protected against any attempt at theft or manipulation.
