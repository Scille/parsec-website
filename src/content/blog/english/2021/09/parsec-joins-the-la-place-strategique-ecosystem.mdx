🚩**Press** release**09/16/2021**

On September 16, Place Stratégique revealed the winners of its second promotion.

The event attracted a full house, and was attended by numerous personalities from the defense and civilian sectors.

Selected on the basis of their technological potential and their initial successes, these young companies have joined a program designed to accelerate their development and growth in France and beyond. They join a number of companies from the first batch who have asked for their support to be extended.

Winners: [PARSEC](https://parsec.cloud/en/) - [Cailabs](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cailabs/) - [H2X-ECOSYSTEMS](https://www.linkedin.com/company/h2x-ecosystems/) - [Inbolt](https://www.linkedin.com/company/inbolt/) - [Lucine](https://www.linkedin.com/company/projet-lucine/) - [OLEDCOMM](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oledcomm/) - [Rnest by La mètis](https://www.linkedin.com/company/la-m-tis/) - [S2P - Smart Plastic Products](https://www.linkedin.com/company/s-2p/) - [TERNWAVES](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ternwaves/)

A pioneer in France in the field of sovereign issues, [La Place Stratégique](https://www.linkedin.com/company/laplacestrategique/) combines civilian, military and entrepreneurial experience, which it mobilizes for the success of young strategic companies. It federates an expert community of institutional, industrial, legal and financial players who contribute to the development of the companies it supports, and work to preserve most of their strategic value in France.
