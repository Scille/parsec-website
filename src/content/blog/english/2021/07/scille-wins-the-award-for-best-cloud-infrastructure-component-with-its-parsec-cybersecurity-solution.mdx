Five companies were honoured this year for the quality and relevance of their solutions and customer implementations:

- **Trophy for Best Cloud Infrastructure Component: [SCILLE](https://parsec.cloud/a-propos-parsec/)**
- Best Customer Case Award: OGUSTINE with EKOKLEAN
- Trophy for best SaaS solution: DATA LEGAL DRIVE
- Start-up of the Year Award: WIZYVISION
- Coup de Coeur Trophy: TEREGA

<OptimizedImage src="eurocloud-trophies-2021-winners.jpg" alt="" />

Declaration of the winners :

**Thierry Leblond, CEO of Scille**, states: _"Parsec's mission is to ensure the "Security & Ergonomics" compromise in "ZeroTrust" mode. Parsec guarantees data confidentiality and integrity in the cloud, while remaining simple to use for the end user. In particular, Parsec provides anti-ransomware data protection, thanks to its historization and traceability features."_
