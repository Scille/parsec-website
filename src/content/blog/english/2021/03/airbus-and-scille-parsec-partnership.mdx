On May 15, 2019, [SCILLE/PARSEC](https://scille.eu/) won the "Paris Cyber week 2019" award presented by AIRBUS Développement at the [Paris Cyber Week](https://systematic-paris-region.org/evenement/paris-cyber-week/) event organized by Systématic Paris région.

As an extension of this award, SCILLE benefits from technical support and financial backing from Airbus Développement. This partnership is established for a period of three years.

This collaboration represents a new opportunity for our [PARSEC](https://parsec.cloud/en/) solution over the next three years.

We look forward to sharing the fruits of our labors with our users very soon, especially with the imminent arrival of the Android version of PARSEC.
