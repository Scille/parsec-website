**Through its Marketplace, 3DS cloud provider Outscale aims to help "the emergence of high value-added, sovereign PaaS and SaaS offerings" in Europe.**

3DS Outscale's ambition is to provide organizations with a " **hyper-confidence cloud** ".

To this end, the marketplace brings together solutions from software publishers and partners, approved and hosted on the French supplier's secure cloud infrastructure.

Cybersecurity, Big Data, Identity Management, IoT, Industrie 4.0... In total, solutions from nine publishers, including [SCILLE](https://scille.eu/) with our [Parsec](https://parsec.cloud/en/) solution, which you will find in the[cybersecurity](https://marketplace.outscale.com/produit/parsec/) category of the catalog available at [https://marketplace.outscale.com/](https://marketplace.outscale.com/)
