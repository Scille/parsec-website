**[The central IT purchasing agency (CAIH)](https://www.caih-sante.org/)** s mission is to simplify IT and telecoms purchasing for its members, by preparing and managing public procurement contracts**.** The members of this ecosystem can be healthcare establishments, health or medico-social structures, public or private not-for-profit.

Through the market **[ELODI](https://www.caih-sante.org/solution-multiediteurs-elodi)**marketplace, CAIH provides access to a plethora of software products negotiated on the best terms and covering most of the digital domains required by healthcare establishments, such as data security, care production, patient management, support services and more.
