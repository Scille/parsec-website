Thierry LEBLOND, CEO Scille/Parsec, has just published the following article in **Cahiers de la sécurité et de la justice n°55 - IHEMI**

This article brings together the work carried out during the first half of 2021 within the framework  
of a political and ecological think tank addressing the issues of security and  
digital sovereignty. It approaches the subject from four angles: environmental security,  
digital sovereignty of citizen uses, digital sovereignty of economic  
operators and the digital sovereignty of States.
