## Cookie policy used by Scille

### 1. Cookies

A cookie is a file that can be saved on the hard drive of Users' devices when using Parsec. These small files may contain, for example, User preferences.

SCILLE may place cookies on Users' devices in order to allow them to browse under optimal conditions, save User profiles and restore their preferences when they are connected via their usual device, or to carry out statistics.

### 2. Type of cookies used via the Parsec.cloud showcase site

SCILLE uses cookies:

- navigation cookies, which are cookies necessary for the proper technical functioning of Parsec as they allow the optimization of content display from each User's device;
- functional cookies, which are not essential to the proper functioning of Parsec but optimize the experience on the Site by allowing Users to be identified;
- analytical cookies, which allow tracking of Users' browsing for optimization purposes. The information collected notably includes the number of visitors, their origin and details of the pages that have been visited. This information helps SCILLE make improvements to Parsec, and to SCILLE's campaigns.

SCILLE does not use any marketing or direct targeting cookies, or third-party cookies, which allow the sending of commercial messages likely to interest Users based on their browsing history.

Third-party cookies are those sent by trusted third-party partners.

Users must accept the placement of cookies on their device via the banner provided for this purpose when they connect to Parsec.

SCILLE does not retain cookies allowing the traceability of a User beyond 1 month after the first placement on a User's device.

Users may at any time delete cookies from their browsing software and configure it to object to their storage on their device.

SCILLE invites them to refer to the help file of their browsing software to establish the appropriate configuration. Browsing Parsec without accepting cookies may be degraded.

### 3. Type of cookies used via the merchant site sign.parsec.cloud

Presence of functional cookies only: These cookies are necessary for the operation of the site; without them you would not be able to use the full range of services offered by the site. They are placed by Scille and only concern the operation of the site. They allow:

- securing reserved and personal areas of the site, such as your account, through identifiers or time-limited access tokens;
- ensuring the security of the authentication mechanism, notably by limiting robotic or unexpected access attempts;
- facilitating access to reserved and personal areas of the site by remembering your identifiers;
- adapting the presentation of the site to your display preferences (language used, display resolution…);
- facilitating the registration process by remembering its content and progress as it goes.

### 4. Traffic

Traffic data is generated when Users' devices are connected to the internet and to PARSEC.

This data may be used for statistical purposes in order to carry out analyses of Parsec's usage and to improve it by adapting it to Users' needs, or to obtain Users' IP addresses. Traffic data is never used by SCILLE in a way that identifies individuals.
